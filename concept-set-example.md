# Example: Six Distinct Portrait Directions

**Brief:** Create six 9:16 editorial portrait concepts with unusual, premium art direction. Keep the concepts meaningfully different.

1. **The Orchard of Instruments** — A tailored subject in a greenhouse where fruit-bearing branches are grafted with delicate brass instrument mechanisms. Mood: quiet invention.
2. **Museum of Unsent Weather** — A portrait in a monumental archive of suspended clouds, each held in a transparent glass cabinet. Mood: reflective and surreal.
3. **The Tidal Tailor** — Sculptural formalwear on a black-sand shore, with fabric pleats echoing retreating tide lines. Mood: composed power.
4. **A Geography of Light** — A clean studio portrait interrupted by precise bands of refracted daylight passing through architectural prisms. Mood: minimal and contemplative.
5. **The Sleeping Observatory** — A subject among mechanical star charts and botanical specimens in a warm, daylight observatory. Mood: curious, not cosmic cliché.
6. **Ceremony for a Small Planet** — A ceremonial outfit in a vast room built around a single floating seed, with restrained scale contrast. Mood: wonder.

These are premise sketches, not finished image prompts. Expand each into a complete prompt using the prompt architecture and the user's constraints.
