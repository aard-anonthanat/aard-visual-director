# Example: Prompt Revision

## User brief

"Make this portrait more premium and creative. Keep the same person and change the outfit to a modern ivory suit."

## Revision approach

- Preserve the supplied reference image as the source for the subject's recognizable appearance.
- Define what "premium" means visually: fabric quality, tailoring, controlled lighting, composition, and restrained palette.
- Add one original visual hook rather than piling on decorative elements.
- Keep the setting and styling coherent with the chosen concept.

## Example prompt

Create a refined 9:16 cinematic editorial portrait using the supplied reference image as the primary visual reference for the subject. Preserve the subject's recognizable facial identity and visible defining features. Dress the subject in a sharply tailored ivory wool suit with a sculpted shoulder line, clean lapels, and subtle tonal texture. Place the subject in a quiet architectural space where a narrow ribbon of daylight passes through a translucent stone screen, casting a precise pattern across the background without obscuring the face. Use a composed three-quarter pose, natural expression, and elegant negative space. Keep the palette to ivory, warm limestone, and soft charcoal accents. Render believable skin, fabric, and stone textures with controlled highlights, gentle shadow depth, and sophisticated editorial photography. Avoid excessive accessories, visual clutter, artificial skin smoothing, and unrelated changes to the subject.
